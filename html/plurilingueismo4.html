This time, we are going to collaborate with our teachers in the organisation of a Solidarity Race. To do this, we will follow the phases proposed previously, which will guide us throughout the process.

Before the Event
Phase 1. Design and Activities to be Carried Out
On this occasion, we are going to organise a Solidarity Race with the association Save the Children. We will request a number of race bibs depending on the number of students participating and we will raise funds to improve the living conditions of children in vulnerable situations around the world.

In addition, we will need to determine a series of factors that must be taken into account to ensure the successful organisation of the event:

Main Goals
To raise awareness about situations of inequality and vulnerability around the world.
To promote physical activity and healthy lifestyle habits among participants.
To encourage solidarity, teamwork, and positive coexistence through a common goal.
Contents or Basic Knowledge
Active and healthy lifestyle:
Benefits of physical activity for health, intensity control, healthy habits such as hydration, warm-up, recovery, etc.

Organisation and management of physical activity:
Planning physical activities, active and autonomous participation, and the safe use of spaces and materials. Students may help organise the route and roles such as volunteers, finish line assistants, etc.

Problem-solving in motor situations:
Effort management, adaptation to the terrain, personal strategies, aerobic endurance training, and improvement of physical fitness.

Social interaction:
Cooperation, respect, teamwork, fair play, inclusive attitudes, and physical activity as a social tool. The aim is to promote solidarity, inclusion regardless of ability level, collective participation, and the involvement of the whole educational community.

Emotional self-regulation:
Improvement of personal achievement, emotional management such as fatigue and frustration, and perseverance in personal goals.

Manifestations of physical culture:
Analysis of the relationship between physical activity and social wellbeing, sports events with social purposes, and the importance of NGOs in the fight against inequality.

Efficient and sustainable interaction with the environment:
Responsible use of the environment, sustainable consumption, and environmental awareness during the event.

Date and Time of the Event
We must consider the activities planned in our area in order to choose a day and time that do not interfere with other events.

Duration of the Event
This aspect must be clearly organised. We need to determine the timetable for each race, if there are several races, and establish a time-control system during the activities to ensure smooth rotations at the agreed times.

Participants
This event will involve all ESO and Bachillerato groups, and one race will be organised for each level. Meanwhile, the remaining participants will take part in alternative sports activities.

Budget
Civil liability insurance, event materials, and prizes to be awarded must be considered.

Additional Services
If we decide to organise the race through the streets of the town, we must inform the authorities about the planned route, the estimated duration of the event, and the number of participating students.

 
Phase 2. Organisation of the Event
In this phase, all the areas involved in the event will be planned: finish line desk, race controllers, finish line controllers, sports referees, scorekeepers, entertainers, and section coordinators.

Each group will be responsible for the necessary materials required to carry out their role. A fast communication system with the main organiser will also be established in order to solve any unexpected problems quickly.

This section will also include the spaces and facilities where the activities will take place, together with an organisational map. The owners or managers of the facilities used for alternative sports activities will be contacted in advance for reservation purposes.

 
Phase 3. Review of Regulations
It is important that any event complies with current regulations, since organisers are legally and administratively responsible for any damage or disorder caused by negligence, lack of prevention, or failure to apply the required safety measures.

Therefore, it will be necessary to analyse the type of event and its characteristics, as well as apply the basic safety regulations that guarantee a safe celebration of the event.

 
Phase 4. Promotion and Publicity of the Event
Finally, in order for the event to achieve the expected success and satisfy the needs of the organising institution, participants, and attendees, proper promotion and publicity must be carried out.

To put this idea into practice, a communication plan should be developed to share and spread information about the event in an organised way through the most appropriate channels: website, social media, traditional media, forums, etc.

 
During the Event
Phase 5. Management of the Sports Event
During this phase, it is necessary for everyone involved in the organisation and participation to know their roles and understand their position within the event planning at all times.

A schedule of actions will be created for the implementation of the event. Organisers will ensure that all human and material resources work smoothly and that the activities run without problems while dealing effectively with any unexpected situations.

Reception of the Public and Participants
This task includes welcoming attendees, whether they are relatives or participants, as well as managing ticket sales, donations, or the sale of drinks and food.

This aspect can improve the experience of both the audience and participants and help make the event a success. Guides or assistants will also be appointed to accompany participating groups to the places assigned in the timetable.

 
After the Event
Phase 6. Analysis and Improvement Proposals
In this phase, we must reflect on the success or failure of the event by evaluating attendance levels, the achievement of the previously established objectives, and the performance of the organisers.

It is also advisable to gather the opinions of participants through questionnaires where they can express their views on the most important aspects of the event, as well as suggestions and improvement proposals for future editions.
