Healthy Habits for Teenagers
Your body is changing very quickly and sometimes you may have doubts about what you should eat or what activities you should do. Now that you are starting to make your own decisions, it is important to know which habits can help you grow in a healthy way.

Healthy Eating
Each person grows at a different rhythm, so it is important to have a balanced diet adapted to your body changes and physical activity.

Remember not to skip meals. You should not drink too many soft drinks or eat too much junk food and pastries. You should also avoid following diets without professional advice.

Social media and advertisements can sometimes promote dangerous diets. Do not believe everything you see online. Look for reliable information and ask health professionals for advice.

Active Free Time
You are still growing, so it is important to stay active. Teenagers should do at least 60 minutes of physical activity every day. This activity can be moderate, like walking or cycling, or vigorous, like running.

You can stay active in many ways: walking to school, using the stairs instead of the lift, playing sports, hiking in the mountains or going for walks in nature with friends.

First Aid
First aid is the emergency help given to a person after an accident or health problem before a doctor arrives. Quick attention can reduce serious consequences.

Emotional Education
Family
Sometimes you may think your parents do not understand you, but they want to help. Talking about your feelings and problems can make you feel better.

Friends
It is good to use social media to talk to friends, but spending time together in person is also important.

Positive Thinking
Sometimes people have negative thoughts about themselves, such as “I am useless”. These thoughts are not realistic. Try to change them into more positive ideas, for example: “Some things are difficult for me, but I am good at other things.”
